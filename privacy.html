Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScopeWeaver Mail ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, uses, stores, and protects information obtained from Google APIs, in particular the Gmail API. ScopeWeaver Mail is a self-hosted email connector component of the ScopeWeaver security-testing platform.

1. Who we are

ScopeWeaver Mail is operated by an individual developer for use in authorized security-testing engagements. Contact: jaybeepy.cyb3rummah@gmail.com. The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC.

2. Information we access

When you authorize the App through Google's OAuth consent screen, the App requests the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, which allows it to read and modify email in the specific mailbox you authorize. This includes:

  • Message headers (From, To, Cc, Date, Subject, Message-ID);
  • Message bodies and attachments you or your agents choose to read;
  • Thread structure, message identifiers, and Gmail labels;
  • Draft messages created by the App.

The App accesses only the mailbox(es) that the operator explicitly authorizes. It does not access any other Google account, service, or data type.

3. How we use the information

Information is used solely to provide the App's features to the account owner:

  • Searching and reading messages and threads;
  • Applying, removing, and updating labels;
  • Archiving, trashing, or marking messages as read/unread;
  • Creating and managing drafts.

4. Limited Use disclosure

ScopeWeaver Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, and consistent with the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless: (a) we have your affirmative agreement for specific messages; (b) it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse; (c) it is required by applicable law; or (d) the data has been aggregated and anonymized.
  • We transfer Google user data to third parties only as necessary to provide or improve the App's features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets with prior notice to you.

5. Storage and security

The App is self-hosted on infrastructure controlled by the operator. OAuth refresh tokens and any cached message data are stored on that infrastructure with restricted file permissions. Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed only as needed. We do not operate a shared multi-tenant service and do not store your data on third-party servers.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share Google user data with third parties except as described in Section 4 (Limited Use) and in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your direction, when your agents act on your behalf within the App;
  • To comply with a valid legal request or applicable law;
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the public.

7. Retention and deletion

Cached message data is retained only as long as needed to provide the App's features and is deleted when no longer needed or upon request. You may request deletion of all data associated with your account at any time by contacting us at the address above.

8. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately invalidates the App's tokens.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or our data practices: jaybeepy.cyb3rummah@gmail.com.
